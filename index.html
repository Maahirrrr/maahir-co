Autonomous Invoice Parsing & Ledger Integration

The Accounts Payable Engine

The Bottleneck: Traditional accounting desks waste hundreds of billable hours manually transcribing unstructured vendor invoices, leading to human error and delayed month-end closing.

The Architecture: We built a proprietary ingestion pipeline utilizing advanced OCR and strict-schema LLM extraction. The engine parses messy PDFs in seconds, structuring the data into clean, validated JSON ready for direct integration into Tally or QuickBooks.

✓
Sub-3-second LLM inference and data extraction.
✓
100% Zero-Data Retention architecture for total compliance.
✓
Strict JSON schema enforcement to eliminate transcription errors.

Automated Ledger Reconciliation & Anomaly Detection

The Reconciliation Agent

The Bottleneck: Finance teams burn valuable advisory time cross-referencing bank statements against manual ledger entries to find missing cents and mismatched records.

The Architecture: A secure, autonomous rules-engine designed to scan raw financial logs before human review. It cross-references data streams to flag discrepancies, duplicate invoices, and compliance risks, outputting a prioritized dashboard for senior review.

✓
Proactive anomaly and duplicate invoice flagging.
✓
Fully isolated data processing to protect sensitive financial logs.
✓
Scalable architecture capable of batch-processing high-volume ledgers.
